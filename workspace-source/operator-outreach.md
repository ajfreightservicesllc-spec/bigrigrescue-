# Operator Outreach — Referral Agreement Pitch

Goal: convert the 8 listed operators into (a) verified listings and
(b) signed referral agreements (10–15% of first-year contract value on closed
members, tracked by lead_id). Send from your own Gmail, one at a time, with the
personalized opener. Keep it short — operators get pitched constantly; what
makes this one different is that they're **already listed free** and you're
offering **leads, not asking for money**.

---

**Subject options (pick one):**
- Your [Space Name] listing on Huntsville Workspaces
- Sending workspace leads your way — [Space Name]
- New Huntsville workspace directory — you're already on it

## Cadence

1. **Day 0:** send the email (Tue–Thu morning lands best).
2. **Day 3–4:** short bump — "Floating this up — happy to fix anything on your
   listing either way."
3. **Week 2:** visit in person. Small market; one coffee beats five emails.
   Bring a one-page agreement.
4. Track status per operator (sent / replied / agreed / signed) in a tab of
   your Leads spreadsheet.

## The one-page agreement (key terms to include)

- Referral fee: [10–15]% of collected fees for the member's first 12 months.
- Attribution: leads identified by Huntsville Workspaces lead_id (email
  forward) or caller mention; disputes resolved against the lead ledger.
- Reporting: operator confirms closed referrals monthly; payment net-15 after
  the member's payment.
- No exclusivity, either side may end with 30 days' notice (existing referrals
  still payable).
- Listing stays free regardless.
